# കുഞ്ഞിയുടെ ഇംഗ്ലീഷ് റേസിംഗ് യാത്ര 🐹🏎️ (Kunji's English Racing Adventure)

An interactive, bilingual English conversation learning web app designed specially for **8-year-old Kunji**, featuring **Hamsters & Race Cars**, Malayalam (മലയാളം) explanations, spoken audio narration, personalized cheers on right answers, and persistent per-topic progress tracking!

Based on 11 spoken English chapters (51 topics, 239 dialogues):
1. 🏎️ **Good Manners** (നല്ല ശീലങ്ങൾ)
2. 🚗 **Going to Public Places** (പൊതുസ്ഥലങ്ങളിലേക്ക് പോകാം)
3. 🐹 **Social Interaction** (കൂട്ടുകാരോട് സംസാരിക്കാം)
4. 🛒 **Shopping** (സാധനങ്ങൾ വാങ്ങാം)
5. ✈️ **Air Travel** (വിമാനയാത്ര)
6. 🏎️💨 **Car & Flat Rental** (കാറും വീടും വാടകയ്‌ക്കെടുക്കാം)
7. 🏨 **Staying in a Hotel** (ഹോട്ടലിൽ താമസിക്കാം)
8. 🩺 **Seeing a Doctor** (ഡോക്ടറെ കാണാം)
9. 🍔 **Eating Out** (റെസ്റ്റോറന്റിൽ ഭക്ഷണം കഴിക്കാം)
10. 🚨 **Solving Problems & Emergencies** (പ്രശ്നങ്ങളും അത്യാഹിതങ്ങളും നേരിടാം)
11. 📱 **Telephoning** (ഫോണിൽ സംസാരിക്കാം)

---

## 🌟 Key Features

1. **Malayalam Translations & Explanations (മലയാളം)**:
   - Full Malayalam script for all 239 conversational sentences.
   - Child-friendly tips and notes explaining context, polite phrases, and vocabulary.
2. **Hamsters & Race Cars Theme 🐹🏎️**:
   - Designed for Kunji's favorite themes: race tracks, speedometers, nitro boosts, cute hamsters with racing helmets, and checkered flags!
3. **Personalized Kunji Appreciations**:
   - Enthusiastic cheers whenever Kunji answers correctly:
     - *"Good job Kunji! 🐹🏎️💨"*
     - *"Great, well done Kunji! 🏆🌟"*
     - *"Super fast Kunji! 🚀💨"*
     - *"Kunji is a champion! 🥇🐹"*
     - *"Awesome driving, Kunji! 🏎️✨"*
   - Spoken audio voice praise + upbeat musical chime + confetti!
4. **Persistent Per-Topic Progress Bar**:
   - Every single topic has its own live progress bar (`3 / 5 വാക്യങ്ങൾ (60%)`).
   - Progress is automatically saved in `localStorage`.
   - Kunji can close the browser anytime, come back days later, and pick up right where he left off!
5. **Interactive Speech Audio**:
   - Tap any bubble or button to hear natural English pronunciation.
   - Speed toggle: Normal (1.0x 🏎️), Gentle (0.85x 🐹), or Slow (0.7x 🐢).

---

## 🚀 How to Run

- **Mac Safari**: Double-click `Open_Kunji_English.command` to open directly in Safari.
- **Any Browser**: Double-click `index.html` or `Kunji_English.html` to open in Safari, Chrome, Edge, or Firefox.
- **iPad / iPhone**: AirDrop or copy `index.html` to iPad, open in Safari, tap Share -> "Add to Home Screen" to use like a native app!
